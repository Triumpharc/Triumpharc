Case study

Reimagine your brand & website.

A full brand identity and portfolio-first website for an interior design studio, built to turn browsers into booked consultations.

Project details

Dream Doors is an interior design studio known for thoughtful, livable spaces — but their online presence didn't reflect that. They came to us without a clear visual identity, and a website that undersold the quality of their work. We built both from the ground up: a warm, confident brand identity, including logo, color palette, and typography, paired with a portfolio-first website that lets the design work speak for itself.

The site is fully responsive, fast-loading, and built around an enquiry flow designed to turn browsing into booked consultations. Rather than burying contact details in a footer, every project page invites the visitor to take the next step at the exact moment they're most interested.

Project research

Before any design work began, we studied how people actually shop for interior design services — mostly on mobile, mostly through images, and mostly comparing a handful of studios before ever picking up the phone. That shaped two early decisions: the site had to be visual-first, and getting in touch had to be one tap away from any project photo, not buried in a footer menu.

We also looked at what comparable studios were doing online, and most leaned on generic templates and stock photography. That gave Dream Doors an easy opening to stand out with a distinct, considered visual identity instead of blending into the same look every competitor was using.

Project results

The new brand and website launched to immediate, visible impact. Enquiries began coming in within the first week of going live, and the studio reports that new clients now regularly reference specific projects from the site during their first conversation. Time spent browsing the site increased noticeably compared to the old one, and the enquiry form sees steady submissions directly from project pages rather than only the contact page.

The clean brand identity has also carried over into business cards, signage, and social presence, giving Dream Doors a consistent look everywhere a potential client encounters them for the first time. What started as a website request became a full brand foundation the studio can build on for years, not just a one-off launch.
